# San Diego Institute of Technology — crawler policy.
#
# These rules were previously injected by Cloudflare's managed robots.txt and
# would have been lost when the site moved to CloudFront. They live here now so
# the policy is the Institute's own: version-controlled, and portable between
# hosts rather than rented from whichever CDN sits in front.
#
# The position: the curriculum is open to read, and open to find. Search engines
# and readers are welcome. Bulk collection for model training is a separate act
# from reading, and it is the one the Institute reserves.

# Content signals (content-signals.org). Any restriction expressed here is an
# express reservation of rights under Article 4 of EU Directive 2019/790.
#
#   search   — building a search index, returning links and short excerpts
#   ai-input — feeding content to a model at query time (RAG, AI search answers)
#   ai-train — training or fine-tuning models

User-agent: *
Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-train=no, use=reference
Allow: /
