Projects · The work

What the Institute makes.

The Institute builds things and puts them in public while they are being built. This page lists them and says where each one stands.

There are four today. Lectio is the first and the largest.

LectioThe first project

The great books, in editions people can read.

The great works are free and almost nobody reads them. The free copy of each is a translation a century or more old.

Lectio gives each book a new edition in plain current English, faithful to the original, and keeps the historical translations and the original language beside it. Seventeen editions exist today, all in beta. Each has its own page, with how it begins, the choices it makes, and the record of how it was drafted and checked.

The reading app is built and not yet released. The plan is about twenty books at launch, then one new book each Monday from January 4, 2027.
