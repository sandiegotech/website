They are the first thing the Institute teaches in public, ahead of its first programs. No background is required.

PerspectivesThe first layer

Free lessons, open to everyone.

The Institute teaches in three layers. The first is Perspectives: free, standalone lessons that anyone can attend. The second is short intensive programs. The third is the degree. The layers open in that order, and this is where it starts.

Each session takes one subject the age makes urgent and teaches it the way the Institute teaches everything: plainly, through real examples, with the aim of leaving you more capable rather than more dependent. The philosophy behind that is public. So is the curriculum.
