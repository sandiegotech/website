The Divine Comedy, Paradiso: Canto XXII

Paradiso: Canto XXII89 of 100

Stunned, with my wits overwhelmed, I turned
to my guide, the way a small child runs
to the one place it trusts the most;

and she, like a mother who comes at once
to her pale and breathless boy and steadies him
with the voice she knows will calm him,

said to me: "Don't you know you're in heaven?
Don't you know that all of heaven is holy,
and that all done here springs from good zeal?

How the singing would have changed you,
and my smiling too — you can imagine now,
since a single cry has shaken you so much;

and in that cry, had you understood its prayers,
you would already know the vengeance
you will live to see before you die.

The sword up here does not strike in haste
or late, however it may seem to the one
who waits for it in fear or in longing.

But turn now, and look toward the others,
for you will see spirits of great renown
if you direct your sight the way I say."

As it pleased her, I turned my eyes back,
and saw a hundred small spheres that together
made each other lovelier with their mingled rays.

I stood like a man who holds inside himself
the sharp point of his wish, and does not dare
to ask — so much he fears to ask too much;

and the largest and most luminous
of those pearls came forward, to satisfy
the wish I held back to know who it was.

Then from within it I heard: "If you could see,
as I do, the love that burns among us,
your unspoken thoughts would all be spoken.

But so that waiting won't make you late
to the high goal, I'll give my answer
even to the thought you guard so closely.

That mountain on whose slope Cassino stands
was once frequented, up at its summit,
by a deluded and ill-disposed people;

and I am the one who first carried up there
the name of Him who brought down to earth
the truth that lifts us so far above ourselves;

and such great grace shone down on me
that I drew the towns around me away
from the godless worship that had seduced the world.

These other fires were every one of them
men of contemplation, kindled by that heat
out of which holy flowers and fruits spring up.

Here is Macarius, here Romualdus,
here are my brothers, who within the cloisters
held their feet steady and their hearts firm."

And I said to him: "The warmth you show me,
speaking with me, and the good face
I see and mark in all your burning lights,

have so opened wide my confidence
as the sun opens the rose, when it unfolds
as fully as it has the power to do.

So I ask you — and you, father, assure me
if I may be given grace enough — that I
may look on you with your face unveiled."

And he: "Brother, your high desire
will be fulfilled in the last sphere of all,
where all the others, and mine, are fulfilled.

There every longing is perfect, ripe,
and whole; in that one sphere alone
is each part right where it has always been,

for it is not in space, it turns on no poles,
and our stairway reaches all the way up to it —
which is why it steals like this from your sight.

Up to that height the patriarch Jacob saw it
reach with its highest rungs, that hour
when it appeared to him so thronged with angels.

But to climb it now, no one lifts
his feet from the earth, and my Rule
stays down below — a dead letter, a waste of paper.

The walls that used to be an abbey
have turned to dens of thieves, and the cowls
are sacks stuffed full of rotten meal.

But not even cruel usury offends
God's pleasure as much as that profit
which drives the hearts of monks so mad;

for whatever the Church holds in trust
belongs to the poor who ask it in God's name,
not to one's kin, or to something worse.

The flesh of mortals is so soft and weak
that down here a good beginning is not enough
to last from the oak's sprouting to its first acorn.

Peter began with neither gold nor silver,
and I with prayer and with fasting,
and Francis with humility built his order;

and if you look at where each of them started,
and then look at where it has run off to,
you'll see the white turned brown.

Truly, Jordan driven backward,
and the sea fleeing when God willed it, were
more a marvel to behold than rescue here would be."

So he spoke to me, and then drew back
into his company, and the company closed up;
then, like a whirlwind, the whole of it swept upward.

The gentle Lady, with a single sign,
pushed me on behind them up that stair,
so did her power overcome my nature;

and never down here, where we go up and down
by nature's law, was any motion so swift
that it could be set beside my flight.

Reader — so may I one day return
to that devout triumph, for whose sake I often
weep for my sins and beat my breast —

you could not have put your finger in the fire
and drawn it out again, before I saw
the sign that follows Taurus, and was inside it.

O glorious stars, O light charged full
with mighty power, from which I trace
all of my genius, whatever it may be,

with you was rising, and hid himself with you,
the one who is father of all mortal life,
when first I breathed the Tuscan air;

and then, when grace was freely given me
to enter the high wheel that turns you round,
your region was allotted to me.

To you, devoutly, at this hour my soul
is sighing, that it may gather the strength
for the hard pass that draws it on toward itself.

"You are so near the final blessedness,"
Beatrice began, "that you ought now
to keep your eyes clear and sharp;

and so, before you go any deeper in,
look down once more, and see how great a world
I have already set beneath your feet;

so that your heart, as glad as it can be,
may show itself to the triumphant throng
that comes rejoicing through this rounded sky."

With my sight I returned through every one
of the seven spheres, and I saw this globe
so small that I smiled at its sorry look;

and that judgment I approve as best
which counts it least; and whoever fixes his mind
on something else may truly be called just.

I saw the daughter of Latona ablaze
without that shadow which once had made me
believe she was thin in some parts and thick in others.

The face of your son, Hyperion,
I could bear here, and I saw how Maia
and Dione move around him and nearby.

From there appeared the tempering of Jove
between his father and his son; and now it was clear,
the way they shift and change their places;

and all seven of them showed me plainly
how vast they are, and how swift they are,
and how far apart their dwellings lie.

The little threshing-floor that makes us so fierce —
while I wheeled round with the eternal Twins —
showed itself to me whole, from its hills to its harbors;

then I turned my eyes back to the eyes I love.
