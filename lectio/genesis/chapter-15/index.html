Genesis, Chapter 15

Count the Stars

Chapter 1515 of 50

After these things the word of the LORD came to Abram in a vision: “Don't be afraid, Abram. I am your shield. Your reward will be very great.” And Abram said, “Lord GOD, what can you give me? I go on childless, and the heir of my house is Eliezer of Damascus.” And Abram said, “Look — you have given me no offspring, and a member of my household will be my heir.” And there it was, the word of the LORD to him: “That man will not be your heir. One who comes out of your own body will be your heir.” And he brought him outside and said, “Look up at the sky and count the stars, if you can count them.” And he said to him, “That is how many your offspring will be.” And he trusted the LORD, and the LORD counted it to him as righteousness.

And he said to him, “I am the LORD, who brought you out of Ur of the Chaldeans to give you this land to possess.” And he said, “Lord GOD, how am I to know that I will possess it?” And he said to him, “Bring me a heifer three years old, a female goat three years old, a ram three years old, a turtledove, and a young pigeon.” And he brought him all of these and cut them in two down the middle, and laid each half opposite the other; the birds he did not cut. And when birds of prey came down on the carcasses, Abram drove them off.

And as the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell on Abram — and there it was: a dread, a great darkness, falling on him. And he said to Abram, “Know this for certain: your offspring will be strangers in a land not their own, and they will be enslaved there and oppressed for four hundred years. But I will judge the nation they serve, and afterward they will come out with great possessions. And you will go to your fathers in peace; you will be buried at a good old age. And in the fourth generation they will come back here — because the wrongdoing of the Amorites is not yet complete.”

And when the sun had gone down and it was dark — there it was: a smoking fire pot and a flaming torch passing between the pieces. On that day the LORD cut a covenant with Abram: “To your offspring I give this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the river Euphrates — the Kenites, the Kenizzites, the Kadmonites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Rephaim, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Girgashites, and the Jebusites.”
