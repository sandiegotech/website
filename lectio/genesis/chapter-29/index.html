Genesis, Chapter 29

Jacob Meets His Match

Chapter 2929 of 50

Then Jacob picked up his feet and went to the land of the people of the east. And he looked, and there was a well in the open country, and three flocks of sheep lying beside it, because the flocks were watered from that well. And the stone over the mouth of the well was large. When all the flocks were gathered there, the shepherds would roll the stone off the mouth of the well and water the sheep, and put the stone back in its place over the mouth of the well. And Jacob said to them, “My brothers, where are you from?” And they said, “We are from Haran.” And he said to them, “Do you know Laban son of Nahor?” And they said, “We know him.” And he said to them, “Is it well with him?” And they said, “It is well — and here is Rachel his daughter coming with the sheep.” And he said, “Look, the day is still high. It isn't time to gather the livestock. Water the sheep and go, graze them.” And they said, “We can't, until all the flocks are gathered and they roll the stone off the mouth of the well. Then we water the sheep.”

While he was still speaking with them, Rachel came with her father's sheep — she was a shepherd. And when Jacob saw Rachel the daughter of Laban his mother's brother, and the sheep of Laban his mother's brother, Jacob went up and rolled the stone off the mouth of the well and watered the flock of Laban his mother's brother. And Jacob kissed Rachel, and he lifted up his voice and wept. And Jacob told Rachel that he was her father's kinsman, that he was Rebekah's son, and she ran and told her father. And when Laban heard the news about Jacob his sister's son, he ran to meet him and threw his arms around him and kissed him and brought him to his house. And he told Laban all these things. And Laban said to him, “Truly you are my bone and my flesh.” And he stayed with him a full month.

And Laban said to Jacob, “Just because you are my kinsman, should you work for me for nothing? Tell me — what should your wages be?” Now Laban had two daughters. The name of the older was Leah, and the name of the younger was Rachel. And Leah's eyes were soft, but Rachel was shapely and beautiful. And Jacob loved Rachel, and he said, “I will work seven years for you for Rachel, your younger daughter.” And Laban said, “Better that I give her to you than that I give her to another man. Stay with me.” So Jacob worked seven years for Rachel, and they were like a few days in his eyes, because of his love for her.

And Jacob said to Laban, “Give me my wife. My time is finished, and I want to go in to her.” And Laban gathered all the men of the place and made a feast. And in the evening he took Leah his daughter and brought her to him, and he went in to her. And Laban gave Zilpah his slave to his daughter Leah as her slave. And in the morning — there was Leah. And he said to Laban, “What is this you have done to me? Wasn't it for Rachel that I worked for you? Why have you deceived me?” And Laban said, “It is not done that way in our place, to give the younger before the firstborn. Finish out this bridal week, and we will give you the other one too, for the work you will do for me another seven years.”

And Jacob did so, and finished out her week, and Laban gave him Rachel his daughter as a wife. And Laban gave Bilhah his slave to his daughter Rachel as her slave. And he went in to Rachel too, and he loved Rachel more than Leah. And he worked for Laban another seven years.

And the LORD saw that Leah was unloved, and he opened her womb; but Rachel was barren. And Leah conceived and bore a son, and she named him Reuben — because she said, “The LORD has seen my suffering. Now my husband will love me.” And she conceived again and bore a son, and she said, “Because the LORD heard that I am unloved, he has given me this one too.” And she named him Simeon. And she conceived again and bore a son, and she said, “Now, this time, my husband will be joined to me, because I have borne him three sons.” That is why he was named Levi. And she conceived again and bore a son, and she said, “This time I will praise the LORD.” That is why she named him Judah. And she stopped bearing.
