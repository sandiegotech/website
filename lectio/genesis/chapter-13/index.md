# Genesis, Chapter 13

Abram and Lot Part

So Abram went up out of Egypt — he and his wife and everything he had, and Lot with him — into the Negev. Abram was very rich in livestock, in silver, and in gold. And he went by stages from the Negev as far as Bethel, to the place where his tent had been at the start, between Bethel and Ai, to the place of the altar he had made there first. And there Abram called on the name of the LORD. Lot, who was traveling with Abram, also had flocks and herds and tents. And the land could not support both of them living together — their holdings were so great that they could not stay together. And there was quarreling between the herders of Abram's livestock and the herders of Lot's livestock. The Canaanites and the Perizzites were living in the land then.

And Abram said to Lot, “Please, let there be no quarreling between me and you, or between my herders and yours. We are brothers. Isn't the whole land in front of you? Separate yourself from me. If you go left, I'll go right; if you go right, I'll go left.” And Lot looked up and saw the whole plain of the Jordan, that it was well watered everywhere — before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah — like the garden of the LORD, like the land of Egypt, all the way to Zoar. So Lot chose the whole plain of the Jordan for himself, and Lot set out eastward, and they separated from each other. Abram settled in the land of Canaan, and Lot settled among the cities of the plain and pitched his tent as far as Sodom. And the men of Sodom were evil, great sinners against the LORD.

And the LORD said to Abram, after Lot had separated from him, “Look up now from where you are, and look north and south and east and west. All the land you see I will give to you and to your offspring forever. And I will make your offspring like the dust of the earth — so that if anyone could count the dust of the earth, then your offspring could be counted too. Get up, walk the length and the breadth of the land, because I am giving it to you.” So Abram moved his tent and came and settled by the oaks of Mamre, at Hebron, and he built an altar there to the LORD.
