# Genesis, Chapter 44

The Silver Cup

And he commanded the one in charge of his house: “Fill the men's packs with food, as much as they can carry, and put each man's silver in the mouth of his pack. And my cup, the silver cup, put in the mouth of the youngest one's pack, with the silver for his grain.” And he did as Joseph told him. At morning light the men were sent off, they and their donkeys. They had left the city and had not gone far when Joseph said to the one in charge of his house, “Get up, chase after the men, and when you catch up with them say to them, ‘Why have you repaid evil for good? Isn't this the one my lord drinks from, and uses for divination? What you have done is evil.’”

And he caught up with them and spoke these words to them. And they said to him, “Why does my lord say such things? Far be it from your servants to do a thing like this. Look, the silver we found in the mouths of our packs we brought back to you from the land of Canaan. How then would we steal silver or gold from your lord's house? Whichever of your servants it is found with, he will die, and we ourselves will be my lord's slaves.” And he said, “Very well, let it be as you say: the one it is found with will be my slave, and the rest of you will be clear.” And each of them hurried to lower his pack to the ground, and each opened his pack. And he searched, beginning with the oldest and ending with the youngest, and the cup was found in Benjamin's pack. And they tore their clothes, and each loaded his donkey, and they went back to the city.

And Judah and his brothers came into Joseph's house — he was still there — and they fell to the ground before him. And Joseph said to them, “What is this thing you have done? Didn't you know that a man like me practices divination?” And Judah said, “What can we say to my lord? What can we speak? How can we clear ourselves? God has found out your servants' guilt. Here we are, my lord's slaves, both we and the one in whose hand the cup was found.” And he said, “Far be it from me to do that. The man in whose hand the cup was found, he will be my slave. As for the rest of you, go up in peace to your father.”

And Judah came near him and said, “Please, my lord, let your servant speak a word in my lord's ears, and do not let your anger flare against your servant, for you are like Pharaoh himself. My lord asked his servants, ‘Do you have a father, or a brother?’ And we said to my lord, ‘We have an old father, and a young child of his old age. His brother is dead, and he alone is left of his mother, and his father loves him.’ And you said to your servants, ‘Bring him down to me, so I may set my eyes on him.’ And we said to my lord, ‘The boy cannot leave his father. If he left his father, his father would die.’ And you said to your servants, ‘Unless your youngest brother comes down with you, you will not see my face again.’ And when we went up to your servant my father, we told him my lord's words. And our father said, ‘Go back, buy us a little food.’ And we said, ‘We cannot go down. If our youngest brother is with us, we will go down; for we cannot see the man's face unless our youngest brother is with us.’ And your servant my father said to us, ‘You know that my wife bore me two sons. One went out from me, and I said: Surely he is torn, torn to pieces. And I have not seen him since. If you take this one from me too, and harm comes to him, you will bring my gray head down to Sheol in misery.’

“And now, if I come to your servant my father and the boy is not with us — and his life is bound up with the boy's life — then when he sees that the boy is not there, he will die, and your servants will bring the gray head of your servant our father down to Sheol in grief. For your servant became a pledge for the boy to my father, saying, ‘If I do not bring him back to you, I will bear the blame before my father all my days.’ So now, please, let your servant stay instead of the boy as my lord's slave, and let the boy go up with his brothers. For how can I go up to my father if the boy is not with me? Let me not see the evil that would find my father.”
