Genesis, Chapter 7

The Flood

Chapter 77 of 50

And the LORD said to Noah, “Go into the ark, you and your whole household, because you are the one I have found righteous in front of me in this generation. Take with you seven pairs of every clean animal, male and female, and one pair of every animal that is not clean, male and female — and seven pairs of the birds of the sky as well, male and female, to keep offspring alive over the face of the whole earth. Because in seven days I will send rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights, and I will wipe every living thing I have made off the face of the ground.” And Noah did it. Everything the LORD commanded him, that is what he did.

Noah was six hundred years old when the flood of waters came over the earth. And Noah went into the ark, with his sons and his wife and his sons' wives, to get away from the waters of the flood. From the clean animals and the animals that are not clean, from the birds and everything that crawls on the ground, they came to Noah two by two into the ark, male and female, as God had commanded Noah. And after seven days the waters of the flood came over the earth.

In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month — on that very day all the springs of the great deep burst open, and the windows of the sky were opened. And the rain fell on the earth forty days and forty nights. On that same day Noah went into the ark, and Shem and Ham and Japheth, Noah's sons, and Noah's wife and the three wives of his sons with them — they, and every wild animal after its kind, and all the livestock after their kind, and every crawling thing that crawls on the earth after its kind, and every bird after its kind, every winged thing of every sort. They came to Noah into the ark, two by two, from all flesh with the breath of life in it. The ones that came in came in male and female, from all flesh, as God had commanded him. And the LORD shut him in.

And the flood came over the earth for forty days. The waters rose and lifted the ark, and it was raised high above the earth. The waters overpowered everything and rose greatly over the earth, and the ark drifted on the surface of the waters. The waters overpowered everything on the earth beyond measure, until all the high mountains under the whole sky were covered. Fifteen cubits above them the waters rose, and the mountains were covered.

And all flesh that moved on the earth died — birds, livestock, wild animals, every swarming thing that swarms on the earth, and every human being. Everything with the breath of life in its nostrils, everything on dry land, died. And he wiped out every living thing that was on the face of the ground — human beings and animals and crawling things and birds of the sky. They were wiped off the earth. Only Noah was left, and those with him in the ark. And the waters overpowered the earth for a hundred and fifty days.
