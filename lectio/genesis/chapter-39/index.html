Genesis, Chapter 39

In the Egyptian's House

Chapter 3939 of 50

And Joseph was taken down to Egypt. And Potiphar, a court official of Pharaoh, the captain of the guard, an Egyptian man, bought him from the hand of the Ishmaelites who had brought him down there. And the LORD was with Joseph, and he was a man who succeeded, and he was in the house of his Egyptian master. And his master saw that the LORD was with him, and that everything he did the LORD made succeed in his hand. And Joseph found favor in his eyes and attended him. And he put him in charge of his house, and everything he had he put in his hand. And from the time he put him in charge of his house and of everything he had, the LORD blessed the Egyptian's house because of Joseph, and the blessing of the LORD was on everything he had, in the house and in the field. And he left everything he had in Joseph's hand, and with him there he gave no thought to anything except the bread he ate. Now Joseph was well built and beautiful.

And after these things his master's wife raised her eyes to Joseph and said, “Lie with me.” And he refused. He said to his master's wife, “Look, with me here my master gives no thought to anything in the house, and everything he has he has put in my hand. No one is greater in this house than I am, and he has held back nothing from me except you, because you are his wife. How could I do this great evil, and sin against God?” And though she spoke to Joseph day after day, he would not listen to her, to lie beside her, to be with her. And on one such day he came into the house to do his work, and none of the men of the household was there in the house. And she caught hold of him by his garment and said, “Lie with me.” And he left his garment in her hand and fled, and went outside.

And when she saw that he had left his garment in her hand and fled outside, she called to the men of her household and said to them, “See — he brought us a Hebrew man to play with us. He came in to me to lie with me, and I screamed. And when he heard me raise my voice and scream, he left his garment beside me and fled, and went outside.” And she kept his garment beside her until his master came home. And she told him the same story: “The Hebrew slave you brought us came in to me to play with me. And when I raised my voice and screamed, he left his garment beside me and fled outside.”

And when his master heard the words his wife spoke to him — “This is what your slave did to me” — his anger flared. And Joseph's master took him and put him in the prison, the place where the king's prisoners were confined. And he was there in the prison. And the LORD was with Joseph and showed him kindness, and gave him favor in the eyes of the warden of the prison. And the warden put all the prisoners in the prison in Joseph's hand, and whatever was done there, he was the one who did it. The warden did not look into anything at all that was in his hand, because the LORD was with him; and whatever he did, the LORD made succeed.
