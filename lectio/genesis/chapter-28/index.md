# Genesis, Chapter 28

The Ladder at Bethel

And Isaac called Jacob and blessed him, and gave him this charge: “Do not take a wife from the Canaanite women. Get up, go to Paddan-aram, to the house of Bethuel your mother's father, and take a wife from there, from the daughters of Laban your mother's brother. And may El Shaddai bless you and make you fruitful and multiply you, so that you become a company of peoples. And may he give you the blessing of Abraham — to you and to your offspring with you — so that you take possession of the land where you have lived as a stranger, which God gave to Abraham.” And Isaac sent Jacob off, and he went to Paddan-aram, to Laban son of Bethuel the Aramean, the brother of Rebekah, the mother of Jacob and Esau.

And Esau saw that Isaac had blessed Jacob and sent him off to Paddan-aram to take a wife there, and that when he blessed him he had charged him, “Do not take a wife from the Canaanite women,” and that Jacob had listened to his father and his mother and gone to Paddan-aram. And Esau saw that the Canaanite women were bad in the eyes of Isaac his father. So Esau went to Ishmael and took Mahalath daughter of Ishmael son of Abraham, the sister of Nebaioth, as a wife, in addition to the wives he had.

And Jacob went out from Beersheba and set out for Haran. And he came upon a certain place and stopped there for the night, because the sun had set. And he took one of the stones of that place and put it under his head, and lay down in that place. And he dreamed — and there was a stairway set on the earth with its top reaching the sky, and there were messengers of God going up and coming down on it. And there was the LORD, standing over him, and he said, “I am the LORD, the God of Abraham your father and the God of Isaac. The land you are lying on I will give to you and to your offspring. And your offspring will be like the dust of the earth, and you will spread out to the west and the east and the north and the south. And in you and in your offspring all the families of the earth will be blessed. And look — I am with you, and I will keep you wherever you go, and I will bring you back to this land. I will not leave you until I have done what I have promised you.”

And Jacob woke from his sleep and said, “Surely the LORD is in this place — and I did not know it.” And he was afraid, and he said, “How terrifying this place is. This is nothing but the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven.” And Jacob got up early in the morning and took the stone he had put under his head and set it up as a standing stone, and poured oil on the top of it. And he named that place Bethel — though the name of the city had been Luz before.

And Jacob made a vow: “If God will be with me and keep me on this road I am going, and give me bread to eat and clothes to wear, and I come back to my father's house in peace — then the LORD will be my God. And this stone I have set up as a standing stone will be God's house. And of everything you give me, I will give a tenth of it back to you.”
