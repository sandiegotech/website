Genesis, Chapter 20

Abimelech

Chapter 2020 of 50

And Abraham moved on from there toward the Negev and settled between Kadesh and Shur, and he stayed a while in Gerar. And Abraham said of Sarah his wife, “She is my sister.” And Abimelech king of Gerar sent and took Sarah. But God came to Abimelech in a dream at night and said to him, “You are a dead man, because of the woman you have taken. She is a married woman.” Now Abimelech had not gone near her, and he said, “Lord, will you kill a nation even when it is innocent? Didn't he say to me, ‘She is my sister’? And she herself said, ‘He is my brother.’ I did this with a whole heart and clean hands.” And God said to him in the dream, “Yes — I know you did this with a whole heart, and I am the one who kept you from sinning against me. That is why I did not let you touch her. Now give the man's wife back. He is a prophet, and he will pray for you, and you will live. But if you do not give her back, know that you will certainly die — you and everyone who is yours.”

And Abimelech got up early in the morning and called all his servants and told them everything, and the men were terrified. Then Abimelech called Abraham and said to him, “What have you done to us? How did I wrong you, that you brought this great guilt on me and on my kingdom? You have done things to me that should never be done.” And Abimelech said to Abraham, “What were you thinking, to do such a thing?” And Abraham said, “Because I thought, there is surely no fear of God in this place, and they will kill me over my wife. Besides, she really is my sister — my father's daughter, though not my mother's daughter — and she became my wife. And when God made me wander from my father's house, I said to her, ‘This is the kindness you can do for me: wherever we go, say of me, He is my brother.’”

And Abimelech took sheep and cattle and male and female slaves and gave them to Abraham, and he gave him back Sarah his wife. And Abimelech said, “Look — my land is in front of you. Settle wherever you like.” And to Sarah he said, “Look, I have given your brother a thousand pieces of silver. It is a covering over the eyes for everyone who is with you. You are completely cleared.” And Abraham prayed to God, and God healed Abimelech and his wife and his slave women, and they bore children again — because the LORD had shut every womb in Abimelech's household tight, on account of Sarah, Abraham's wife.
