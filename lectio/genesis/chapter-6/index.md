# Genesis, Chapter 6

The Earth Filled With Violence

When human beings began to multiply on the face of the ground and daughters were born to them, the sons of God saw that the daughters of human beings were beautiful, and they took as wives any they chose. And the LORD said, “My breath will not stay in a human being forever, because he is flesh. His days will be a hundred and twenty years.” The Nephilim were on the earth in those days — and afterward too — when the sons of God went in to the daughters of human beings and had children by them. Those were the mighty men of old, the men of renown.

And the LORD saw that human evil was great on the earth, and that every shape the thoughts of the heart took was nothing but evil, all day long. And the LORD was sorry that he had made human beings on the earth, and it grieved him to the heart. And the LORD said, “I will wipe the human beings I created off the face of the ground — human beings and animals and crawling things and birds of the sky. I am sorry that I made them.” But Noah found favor in the eyes of the LORD.

These are the generations of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, blameless among the people of his time, and Noah walked with God. And Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth. The earth was ruined in God's sight, and the earth was filled with violence. And God looked at the earth, and there it was, ruined — because all flesh had ruined its way on the earth.

And God said to Noah, “The end of all flesh has come before me. The earth is filled with violence because of them, and now I am going to ruin them, along with the earth. Make yourself an ark of gopher wood. Make rooms in the ark, and cover it inside and out with pitch. This is how you are to make it: the ark is to be three hundred cubits long, fifty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high. Make a roof for the ark and finish it to a cubit from the top. Put the door of the ark in its side, and make it with lower, second, and third decks.

“And look — I am bringing the flood of waters over the earth, to ruin all flesh with the breath of life in it under the sky. Everything on the earth will die. But I will establish my covenant with you, and you will come into the ark — you and your sons and your wife and your sons' wives with you. And from every living thing, from all flesh, bring two of every kind into the ark to keep them alive with you; they are to be male and female. From the birds after their kind, and the animals after their kind, and every crawling thing of the ground after its kind, two of every kind will come to you to be kept alive. And take with you every kind of food that is eaten, and store it up — it will be food for you and for them.” And Noah did it. Everything God commanded him, that is what he did.
