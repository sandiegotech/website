# Genesis, Chapter 38

Judah and Tamar

And at that time Judah went down from his brothers and turned aside to an Adullamite man whose name was Hirah. And there Judah saw the daughter of a Canaanite man whose name was Shua, and he took her and went in to her. And she conceived and bore a son, and he named him Er. And she conceived again and bore a son, and she named him Onan. And once more she bore a son, and she named him Shelah. He was at Chezib when she bore him. And Judah took a wife for Er his firstborn, and her name was Tamar. But Er, Judah's firstborn, was evil in the eyes of the LORD, and the LORD put him to death. And Judah said to Onan, “Go in to your brother's wife and do a brother-in-law's duty by her, and raise up offspring for your brother.” But Onan knew the offspring would not be his. So whenever he went in to his brother's wife he wasted it on the ground, so as not to give offspring to his brother. And what he did was evil in the eyes of the LORD, and he put him to death too. And Judah said to Tamar his daughter-in-law, “Stay a widow in your father's house until Shelah my son grows up” — because he thought, “He might die too, like his brothers.” So Tamar went and stayed in her father's house.

And many days passed, and Shua's daughter, Judah's wife, died. And when Judah was comforted, he went up to his sheepshearers at Timnah, he and his friend Hirah the Adullamite. And Tamar was told, “Look, your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his sheep.” And she took off her widow's clothes and covered herself with a veil and wrapped herself up, and she sat at the entrance to Enaim, which is on the road to Timnah — because she saw that Shelah had grown up, and she had not been given to him as a wife. And Judah saw her and took her for a whore, because she had covered her face. And he turned aside to her by the road and said, “Come, please, let me come in to you” — because he did not know she was his daughter-in-law. And she said, “What will you give me to come in to me?” And he said, “I will send a kid from the flock.” And she said, “Only if you give a pledge until you send it.” And he said, “What pledge should I give you?” And she said, “Your seal and your cord, and the staff in your hand.” And he gave them to her and went in to her, and she conceived by him. And she got up and went, and she took off her veil and put on her widow's clothes.

And Judah sent the kid by the hand of his friend the Adullamite, to get the pledge back from the woman's hand, and he did not find her. And he asked the men of her place, “Where is the consecrated woman, the one at Enaim by the road?” And they said, “There has been no consecrated woman here.” And he went back to Judah and said, “I did not find her. And the men of the place also said, ‘There has been no consecrated woman here.’” And Judah said, “Let her keep them, or we will be a laughingstock. Look, I sent this kid, and you did not find her.”

And about three months later Judah was told, “Tamar your daughter-in-law has played the whore, and look — she is pregnant by her whoring.” And Judah said, “Bring her out and let her be burned.” As she was being brought out, she sent word to her father-in-law: “By the man these belong to, I am pregnant.” And she said, “Recognize, please, whose these are — the seal and the cords and the staff.” And Judah recognized them and said, “She is more in the right than I am, because I did not give her to Shelah my son.” And he did not know her again.

And when the time came for her to give birth — there were twins in her womb. And as she was giving birth, one put out a hand, and the midwife took a crimson thread and tied it on his hand, saying, “This one came out first.” But as he drew back his hand — out came his brother. And she said, “What a breach you have made for yourself!” And he was named Perez. And afterward his brother came out, the one with the crimson on his hand, and he was named Zerah.
