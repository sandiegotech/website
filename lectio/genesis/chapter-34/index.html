Genesis, Chapter 34

Dinah

Chapter 3434 of 50

And Dinah, the daughter Leah had borne to Jacob, went out to see the women of the land. And Shechem son of Hamor the Hivite, the chief of the region, saw her, and he took her and lay with her and violated her. And he was bound to Dinah, Jacob's daughter, and he loved the girl, and he spoke to the girl's heart. And Shechem said to Hamor his father, “Get me this girl as a wife.” And Jacob heard that he had defiled Dinah his daughter — and his sons were with his livestock in the field, and Jacob said nothing until they came in. And Hamor, Shechem's father, went out to Jacob to speak with him. And Jacob's sons came in from the field when they heard, and the men were sickened, and they burned with anger, because he had done an outrage in Israel by lying with Jacob's daughter — a thing that should not be done.

And Hamor spoke with them: “My son Shechem is set on your daughter. Please give her to him as a wife. And make marriages with us: give us your daughters, and take our daughters for yourselves. And live with us. The land is open in front of you — settle in it, trade in it, get holdings in it.” And Shechem said to her father and her brothers, “Let me find favor in your eyes, and whatever you tell me, I will give. Ask me for as high a bride-price and gift as you like, and I will give whatever you tell me. Only give me the girl as a wife.”

And Jacob's sons answered Shechem and Hamor his father with deceit — because he had defiled Dinah their sister. And they said to them, “We can't do this, give our sister to a man who is uncircumcised. That would be a disgrace to us. Only on this condition will we agree to you: that you become like us, that every male among you is circumcised. Then we will give you our daughters and take your daughters for ourselves, and we will live among you and become one people. But if you will not listen to us and be circumcised, we will take our daughter and go.” And their words were good in the eyes of Hamor and of Shechem son of Hamor. And the young man did not put off doing it, because he wanted Jacob's daughter. And he was the most honored of his father's whole household.

And Hamor and Shechem his son came to the gate of their city and spoke to the men of their city: “These men are at peace with us. Let them settle in the land and trade in it — look, the land is wide enough on both sides for them. Let us take their daughters as wives for ourselves, and give them our daughters. Only on this condition will the men agree to live with us and become one people: that every male among us is circumcised, as they are circumcised. Their livestock and their property and all their animals — won't they be ours? Only let us agree to them, and they will live with us.” And everyone who went out of the gate of his city listened to Hamor and Shechem his son, and every male was circumcised, everyone who went out of the gate of his city.

And on the third day, while they were in pain, two of Jacob's sons, Simeon and Levi, Dinah's brothers, each took his sword and came into the city unopposed and killed every male. And they killed Hamor and Shechem his son with the edge of the sword, and took Dinah out of Shechem's house and left. Jacob's sons came upon the bodies and plundered the city, because they had defiled their sister. They took their flocks and their herds and their donkeys, whatever was in the city and whatever was in the field, and all their wealth. And all their little ones and their wives they took captive, and they plundered everything in the houses.

And Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, “You have brought disaster on me, made me a stench to the people of this land, to the Canaanites and the Perizzites. My men are few. They will band together against me and strike me, and I will be wiped out, I and my household.” And they said, “Should he treat our sister like a whore?”
