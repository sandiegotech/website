Genesis, Chapter 35

Bethel Again, and Rachel's Grave

Chapter 3535 of 50

And God said to Jacob, “Get up, go up to Bethel and stay there, and make an altar there to the God who appeared to you when you were running from Esau your brother.” And Jacob said to his household and to everyone who was with him, “Put away the foreign gods that are among you, and make yourselves clean, and change your clothes. And let us get up and go up to Bethel, and I will make an altar there to the God who answered me on the day of my distress, and who has been with me on the road I have traveled.” And they gave Jacob all the foreign gods that were in their hands, and the rings that were in their ears, and Jacob buried them under the oak that is near Shechem. And they set out. And a terror of God was on the cities around them, and they did not chase after Jacob's sons. And Jacob came to Luz, which is in the land of Canaan — that is, Bethel — he and all the people who were with him. And he built an altar there and called the place El-bethel, because there God had shown himself to him when he was running from his brother. And Deborah, Rebekah's nurse, died, and she was buried below Bethel, under the oak. And it was named Allon-bacuth.

And God appeared to Jacob again, when he came from Paddan-aram, and blessed him. And God said to him, “Your name is Jacob. You will no longer be called Jacob; Israel will be your name.” And he named him Israel. And God said to him, “I am El Shaddai. Be fruitful and multiply. A nation and a company of nations will come from you, and kings will come out of your loins. And the land that I gave to Abraham and to Isaac, I will give to you; and to your offspring after you I will give the land.” And God went up from him, at the place where he had spoken with him. And Jacob set up a standing stone at the place where he had spoken with him, a standing stone of rock, and he poured a drink offering on it, and poured oil on it. And Jacob named the place where God had spoken with him Bethel.

And they set out from Bethel. And when there was still some distance to go to Ephrath, Rachel went into labor, and her labor was hard. And when her labor was at its hardest, the midwife said to her, “Don't be afraid, for this one too is a son for you.” And as her life went out of her — for she was dying — she named him Ben-oni. But his father called him Benjamin. And Rachel died, and she was buried on the road to Ephrath — that is, Bethlehem. And Jacob set up a standing stone over her grave. It is the standing stone of Rachel's grave to this day.

And Israel set out and pitched his tent beyond Migdal-eder. And while Israel was living in that land, Reuben went and lay with Bilhah, his father's concubine. And Israel heard. Now the sons of Jacob were twelve. The sons of Leah: Reuben, Jacob's firstborn, and Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun. The sons of Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin. The sons of Bilhah, Rachel's slave: Dan and Naphtali. The sons of Zilpah, Leah's slave: Gad and Asher. These are the sons of Jacob who were born to him in Paddan-aram.

And Jacob came to Isaac his father at Mamre, at Kiriath-arba — that is, Hebron — where Abraham and Isaac had lived as strangers. And the days of Isaac were a hundred and eighty years. And Isaac breathed his last and died, and was gathered to his people, old and full of days. And Esau and Jacob his sons buried him.
