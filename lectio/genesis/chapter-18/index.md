# Genesis, Chapter 18

Three Visitors, and the Argument

And the LORD appeared to him by the oaks of Mamre, while he was sitting at the entrance of his tent in the heat of the day. He looked up, and there they were: three men standing near him. When he saw them he ran from the tent entrance to meet them and bowed to the ground, and said, “My lord, if I have found favor in your eyes, please don't pass by your servant. Let a little water be brought, and wash your feet, and rest under the tree. And let me bring a piece of bread, so you can steady yourselves — after that you can go on, since you have come by your servant.” And they said, “Do as you have said.” And Abraham hurried into the tent to Sarah and said, “Quick — three measures of fine flour. Knead it and make bread.” And Abraham ran to the herd and took a calf, tender and good, and gave it to a servant, who hurried to prepare it. And he took curds and milk and the calf he had prepared, and set it in front of them; and he stood near them under the tree while they ate.

And they said to him, “Where is Sarah your wife?” And he said, “There, in the tent.” And he said, “I will certainly come back to you at this time next year — and look, Sarah your wife will have a son.” And Sarah was listening at the tent entrance, behind him. Now Abraham and Sarah were old, well along in years, and it had stopped being with Sarah the way it is with women. And Sarah laughed to herself and said, “After I am worn out, and my husband is old, am I to have pleasure?”

And the LORD said to Abraham, “Why did Sarah laugh and say, ‘Am I really going to give birth, old as I am?’ Is anything too wonderful for the LORD? At the appointed time I will come back to you, at this time next year, and Sarah will have a son.” And Sarah denied it, because she was afraid: “I didn't laugh.” And he said, “No — you did laugh.”

And the men got up from there and looked down toward Sodom, and Abraham walked with them to send them off. And the LORD said, “Should I hide from Abraham what I am about to do — seeing that Abraham is going to become a great and mighty nation, and all the nations of the earth will be blessed in him? For I have known him, so that he will command his children and his household after him to keep the way of the LORD by doing what is right and just — so that the LORD can bring about for Abraham what he promised him.” And the LORD said, “The outcry against Sodom and Gomorrah is great, and their sin is very heavy. Let me go down and see whether they have done altogether according to the outcry that has come to me. And if not, I will know.”

And the men turned from there and went toward Sodom, but Abraham was still standing in front of the LORD. And Abraham stepped forward and said, “Are you really going to sweep away the righteous along with the wicked? Suppose there are fifty righteous inside the city — will you really sweep it away, and not spare the place for the sake of the fifty righteous in it? Far be it from you to do such a thing, to kill the righteous with the wicked, so that the righteous and the wicked come to the same end. Far be it from you. Will the Judge of all the earth not do justice?” And the LORD said, “If I find fifty righteous inside the city of Sodom, I will spare the whole place for their sake.”

And Abraham answered, “Look — I have taken it on myself to speak to my Lord, and I am dust and ashes. Suppose the fifty righteous lack five. Will you destroy the whole city over five?” And he said, “I will not destroy it if I find forty-five there.” And he spoke to him again: “Suppose forty are found there.” And he said, “I will not do it, for the sake of the forty.” And he said, “Please don't let my Lord be angry, and I will speak. Suppose thirty are found there.” And he said, “I will not do it if I find thirty there.” And he said, “Look — I have taken it on myself to speak to my Lord. Suppose twenty are found there.” And he said, “I will not destroy it, for the sake of the twenty.” And he said, “Please don't let my Lord be angry, and I will speak just this once. Suppose ten are found there.” And he said, “I will not destroy it, for the sake of the ten.” And the LORD went his way, as soon as he had finished speaking with Abraham. And Abraham returned to his place.
