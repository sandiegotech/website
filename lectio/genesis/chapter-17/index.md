# Genesis, Chapter 17

New Names

When Abram was ninety-nine years old, the LORD appeared to him and said, “I am El Shaddai. Walk in front of me and be whole. And I will make my covenant between me and you, and multiply you greatly.” And Abram fell on his face, and God spoke with him: “As for me — look, my covenant is with you, and you will be the father of a crowd of nations. Your name will no longer be Abram. Your name will be Abraham, because I have made you the father of a crowd of nations. I will make you very fruitful. I will make nations of you, and kings will come out of you. And I will establish my covenant between me and you and your offspring after you, through their generations, as an everlasting covenant — to be God to you and to your offspring after you. And I will give you, and your offspring after you, the land where you are a stranger, all the land of Canaan, as an everlasting possession. And I will be their God.”

And God said to Abraham, “As for you, you are to keep my covenant — you and your offspring after you, through their generations. This is my covenant, which you are to keep, between me and you and your offspring after you: every male among you is to be circumcised. You will circumcise the flesh of your foreskin, and it will be the sign of the covenant between me and you. At eight days old every male among you is to be circumcised, through your generations — one born in your household, or bought with money from any foreigner who is not your offspring. The one born in your household and the one bought with your money must be circumcised. My covenant will be in your flesh as an everlasting covenant. And any uncircumcised male, whose foreskin has not been cut, will be cut off from his people. He has broken my covenant.”

And God said to Abraham, “As for Sarai your wife — you will not call her Sarai. Sarah will be her name. And I will bless her, and I will also give you a son by her. I will bless her, and she will become nations. Kings of peoples will come from her.” And Abraham fell on his face and laughed, and said in his heart, “Can a child be born to a man who is a hundred years old? Can Sarah, who is ninety, give birth?” And Abraham said to God, “If only Ishmael might live in front of you!”

And God said, “No — Sarah your wife will bear you a son, and you will name him Isaac. And I will establish my covenant with him as an everlasting covenant, and with his offspring after him. And as for Ishmael, I have heard you. Look — I have blessed him. I will make him fruitful and multiply him greatly. He will father twelve princes, and I will make him a great nation. But my covenant I will establish with Isaac, whom Sarah will bear to you at this time next year.” And when he had finished speaking with him, God went up from Abraham.

And Abraham took Ishmael his son, and everyone born in his household and everyone bought with his money, every male among the men of Abraham's household, and he circumcised the flesh of their foreskin that very day, as God had told him. Abraham was ninety-nine years old when the flesh of his foreskin was circumcised, and Ishmael his son was thirteen. On that very day Abraham and Ishmael his son were circumcised, and all the men of his household, born in the household and bought with money from a foreigner, were circumcised with him.
