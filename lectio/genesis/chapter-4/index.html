Genesis, Chapter 4

Cain and Abel

Chapter 44 of 50

The man knew Eve his wife, and she conceived and gave birth to Cain. She said, “I have made a man, with the LORD.” Then she gave birth to his brother Abel. Abel kept flocks; Cain worked the ground. In time Cain brought the LORD an offering from the fruit of the ground, and Abel brought some of the firstborn of his flock — the fat parts of them. The LORD looked on Abel and his offering, and on Cain and his offering he did not look. Cain was furious, and his face fell.

The LORD said to Cain, “Why are you furious? Why has your face fallen? If you do well, isn't there a lifting up? And if you don't do well, sin is crouching at the door — and its desire is for you, but you can master it.” And Cain said to his brother Abel — and while they were out in the field, Cain rose up against his brother Abel and killed him.

The LORD said to Cain, “Where is your brother Abel?” He said, “I don't know. Am I my brother's keeper?” And he said, “What have you done? Your brother's blood is crying out to me from the ground. And now you are cursed from the ground, which opened its mouth to take your brother's blood from your hand. When you work the ground, it will no longer give you its strength. You will be a restless wanderer on the earth.”

Cain said to the LORD, “My punishment is more than I can carry. Look — you have driven me today off the face of the ground, and I will be hidden from your face. I'll be a restless wanderer on the earth, and anyone who finds me will kill me.” The LORD said to him, “Then whoever kills Cain will be avenged seven times over.” And the LORD put a mark on Cain, so that no one who found him would strike him down. And Cain went out from the LORD's presence and settled in the land of Nod, east of Eden.

Cain knew his wife, and she conceived and gave birth to Enoch. And Cain was building a city, and he named the city after his son, Enoch. Irad was born to Enoch; Irad fathered Mehujael; Mehujael fathered Methushael; Methushael fathered Lamech. Lamech took two wives — one named Adah, the other Zillah. Adah gave birth to Jabal; he was the father of those who live in tents and keep livestock. His brother's name was Jubal; he was the father of all who play the lyre and the pipe. And Zillah gave birth to Tubal-cain, who forged every kind of tool of bronze and iron. Tubal-cain's sister was Naamah.

And Lamech said to his wives: “Adah and Zillah, hear my voice. Wives of Lamech, listen to what I say. I have killed a man for wounding me, a boy for striking me. If Cain is avenged seven times, then Lamech seventy-seven.”

The man knew his wife again, and she gave birth to a son and named him Seth — “because,” she said, “God has set in place another offspring for me instead of Abel, since Cain killed him.” And to Seth also a son was born, and he named him Enosh. That was when people began to call on the name of the LORD.
