Genesis, Chapter 1

The Seven Days

Chapter 11 of 50

When God began to create the heavens and the earth — the earth was a shapeless waste, darkness lay over the face of the deep, and the breath of God hovered over the waters — God said, “Let there be light.” And there was light. God saw that the light was good, and God separated the light from the darkness. God called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. And there was evening, and there was morning: the first day.

God said, “Let there be a vault in the middle of the waters, to separate water from water.” So God made the vault, and it separated the water beneath it from the water above it. And so it was. God called the vault Sky. And there was evening, and there was morning: the second day.

God said, “Let the waters under the sky be gathered into one place, so that dry land can appear.” And so it was. God called the dry land Earth, and the gathered waters he called Seas. And God saw that it was good. God said, “Let the earth put out growth: seed-bearing plants, and every kind of fruit tree bearing fruit with its seed inside it.” And so it was. The earth put out growth — every kind of seed-bearing plant, every kind of tree bearing fruit with its seed inside it. And God saw that it was good. And there was evening, and there was morning: the third day.

God said, “Let there be lights in the vault of the sky to separate day from night. Let them be signs, and mark the seasons, the days, and the years, and let them shine in the vault of the sky to light the earth.” And so it was. God made the two great lights — the greater light to govern the day, the lesser light to govern the night — and the stars. God set them in the vault of the sky to light the earth, to govern day and night, and to separate light from darkness. And God saw that it was good. And there was evening, and there was morning: the fourth day.

God said, “Let the waters swarm with living creatures, and let birds fly over the earth, across the vault of the sky.” So God created the great sea creatures, and every kind of living thing that moves and swarms in the water, and every kind of winged bird. And God saw that it was good. God blessed them: “Be fruitful and multiply. Fill the waters of the seas — and let the birds multiply on the earth.” And there was evening, and there was morning: the fifth day.

God said, “Let the earth bring forth every kind of living creature: livestock, crawling things, and every kind of wild animal.” And so it was. God made every kind of wild animal, every kind of livestock, and everything that crawls on the ground. And God saw that it was good.

Then God said, “Let us make humanity in our image, after our likeness, and let them hold sway over the fish of the sea, the birds of the sky, the livestock, the whole earth, and everything that crawls on it.” So God created humanity in his own image — in the image of God he created it; male and female he created them. God blessed them, and God said to them, “Be fruitful and multiply. Fill the earth and master it. Hold sway over the fish of the sea, the birds of the sky, and every living thing that moves on the earth.” And God said, “Look — I have given you every seed-bearing plant on the face of the earth, and every tree with seed in its fruit. They are yours to eat. And to every wild animal, every bird of the sky, and everything that crawls on the earth with the breath of life in it, I give every green plant for food.” And so it was. And God saw all that he had made — and look, it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning: the sixth day.
