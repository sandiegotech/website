The Gospel of Mark, Chapter 5

Legion, and the Girl Who Got Up

Chapter 55 of 16

They came to the other side of the sea, to the region of the Gerasenes. As he stepped out of the boat, right away a man with an unclean spirit came out of the tombs to meet him. He lived among the tombs, and nobody could restrain him anymore, not even with a chain. He had often been shackled hand and foot, and he had wrenched the chains apart and smashed the shackles. Nobody was strong enough to subdue him. Night and day, in the tombs and on the hills, he kept screaming and cutting himself with stones.

When he saw Jesus from a distance he ran and fell down in front of him, and screamed at the top of his voice, “What do you want with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? In God's name, don't torture me!” — because Jesus had been saying to him, “Come out of the man, you unclean spirit.” And Jesus asked him, “What is your name?” He said, “My name is Legion — there are many of us.” And he begged Jesus over and over not to send them out of the region. Now a big herd of pigs was feeding there on the hillside, and the spirits begged him, “Send us into the pigs. Let us go into them.” He let them. And the unclean spirits came out and went into the pigs, and the herd — about two thousand of them — stampeded down the bank into the sea and drowned there.

The men herding them ran off and told it in the town and out in the country, and people came to see what had happened. They came to Jesus and saw the man who had been possessed, the one who had had Legion, sitting there dressed and in his right mind — and they were frightened. The ones who had seen it told them what had happened to the man, and about the pigs. And they started begging Jesus to leave their district.

As he was getting into the boat, the man who had been possessed begged to go with him. Jesus wouldn't let him. He said, “Go home to your own people and tell them what the Lord has done for you, and how he had mercy on you.” So the man went off and started telling it all over the Decapolis — what Jesus had done for him. And everyone was amazed.

When Jesus had crossed back over to the other side, a large crowd gathered around him while he was beside the sea. One of the synagogue leaders came — Jairus was his name — and when he saw Jesus he fell at his feet and begged him and begged him: “My little girl is dying. Come and put your hands on her, so she'll be saved and live.” Jesus went with him, and a large crowd followed and pressed in on him.

There was a woman who had been bleeding for twelve years. She had suffered a great deal under many doctors and spent everything she had, and she was no better — she had gotten worse. She had heard about Jesus, and she came up behind him in the crowd and touched his coat, because she was telling herself, “If I can just touch his clothes, I'll be saved.” And right away the flow of her blood dried up, and she felt in her body that she was healed of her affliction.

Right away Jesus knew in himself that power had gone out of him. He turned around in the crowd and said, “Who touched my clothes?” His disciples said to him, “You see this crowd pressing in on you, and you say, ‘Who touched me?’” But he kept looking around to see who had done it. The woman, frightened and shaking, knowing what had happened to her, came and fell down in front of him and told him the whole truth. He said to her, “Daughter, your faith has saved you. Go in peace, and be free of your affliction.”

He was still speaking when people came from the synagogue leader's house and said, “Your daughter is dead. Why bother the teacher any longer?” Jesus overheard what they said and told the synagogue leader, “Don't be afraid. Just keep trusting.” He let no one go with him except Peter, James, and James's brother John. They came to the house, and he saw the commotion — people weeping and wailing loudly. He went in and said to them, “Why all this commotion and crying? The child isn't dead. She's asleep.” And they laughed at him. But he put them all outside, and took the child's father and mother and the ones with him, and went in where the child was.

He took the child by the hand and said to her, “Talitha koum” — which means, “Little girl, I'm telling you, get up.” And right away the girl got up and started walking around; she was twelve years old. And they were completely beside themselves. He gave them strict orders that nobody should know about it, and told them to give her something to eat.
