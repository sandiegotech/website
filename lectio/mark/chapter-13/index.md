# The Gospel of Mark, Chapter 13

Not One Stone Left on Another

As he was going out of the temple, one of his disciples said to him, “Teacher — look at these stones! Look at these buildings!” And Jesus said to him, “You see these great buildings? Not one stone will be left here on another. Every one will be thrown down.” While he was sitting on the Mount of Olives, facing the temple, Peter and James and John and Andrew asked him privately, “Tell us — when will these things happen? And what will be the sign that they're all about to be finished?”

Jesus began to say to them: “Watch out that nobody leads you astray. Many will come using my name and saying, ‘I'm the one,’ and they will lead many astray. When you hear of wars and rumors of wars, don't be alarmed. It has to happen, but it isn't the end yet. Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. There will be earthquakes in place after place; there will be famines. These are the beginning of the birth pains.

“But watch out for yourselves. They will hand you over to the councils, and you'll be beaten in the synagogues, and you'll stand before governors and kings because of me, as a testimony to them. And the good news has to be announced first to all the nations. When they arrest you and hand you over, don't worry beforehand about what to say. Say whatever is given to you at that moment — because it isn't you speaking, it's the Holy Spirit. Brother will hand over brother to death, and a father his child; children will rise against their parents and have them put to death. And you will be hated by everyone because of my name. But whoever holds out to the end will be saved.

“When you see the abomination of desolation standing where it should not” — let the reader understand — “then the people in Judea must run for the hills. Anyone on the roof must not go down or go inside to take anything out of the house. Anyone out in the field must not turn back to get his coat. It will be terrible for women who are pregnant or nursing in those days. Pray that it doesn't happen in winter. Because those days will be a suffering like nothing since the beginning of the creation God made until now — and never will be again. If the Lord had not cut those days short, nobody at all would be saved; but for the sake of the chosen, the ones he chose, he cut those days short.

“And if anyone says to you then, ‘Look, here's the Messiah,’ or, ‘Look, there he is,’ don't believe it. False messiahs and false prophets will appear and produce signs and wonders to lead astray, if possible, even the chosen. So watch out. I've told you everything ahead of time.

“But in those days, after that suffering, the sun will go dark, and the moon won't give its light; the stars will be falling from the sky, and the powers in the heavens will be shaken. And then they will see the Son of Man coming in clouds with great power and glory. And then he will send out the angels and gather his chosen from the four winds, from the ends of the earth to the ends of the sky.

“Learn the lesson of the fig tree. As soon as its branch turns soft and puts out leaves, you know that summer is near. In the same way, when you see these things happening, you know that he is near, right at the door. I'm telling you the truth: this generation will not pass away until all these things have happened. Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away.

“But about that day or that hour nobody knows — not the angels in heaven, not the Son, only the Father. Watch out. Stay awake. You don't know when the time is coming. It's like a man going abroad: he leaves his house and puts his servants in charge, each with his own work, and tells the doorkeeper to stay awake. So stay awake — because you don't know when the master of the house is coming: evening, or midnight, or when the rooster crows, or dawn. He might come suddenly and find you asleep. And what I say to you I say to everyone: stay awake.”
