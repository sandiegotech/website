The Gospel of Mark, Chapter 10

The Road Up to Jerusalem

Chapter 1010 of 16

He got up and left there for the region of Judea, and across the Jordan. Crowds gathered around him again, and as usual he taught them. Some Pharisees came up to test him, and asked whether a man is allowed to divorce his wife. He answered them, “What did Moses command you?” They said, “Moses allowed a man to write a certificate of dismissal and send her away.” Jesus said to them, “He wrote you that command because your hearts are hard. But from the beginning of creation, ‘God made them male and female.’ ‘For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and hold fast to his wife, and the two will become one flesh.’ So they are no longer two — they are one flesh. What God has joined together, no one should separate.”

Back in the house the disciples asked him about this again. He said to them, “Whoever divorces his wife and marries another commits adultery against her. And if she divorces her husband and marries another, she commits adultery.”

People were bringing him little children so he could touch them, and the disciples scolded them. When Jesus saw it he was indignant, and said to them, “Let the children come to me. Don't stop them. The kingdom of God belongs to people like these. I'm telling you the truth: whoever doesn't welcome the kingdom of God the way a child does will never get in.” And he put his arms around them, laid his hands on them, and blessed them.

As he was setting out on the road, a man ran up, knelt in front of him, and asked him, “Good teacher, what do I have to do to inherit eternal life?” Jesus said to him, “Why do you call me good? Nobody is good but God alone. You know the commandments: don't murder, don't commit adultery, don't steal, don't give false testimony, don't defraud, honor your father and mother.” He said to him, “Teacher, I've kept all of these since I was a boy.” Jesus looked straight at him and loved him, and said, “You're short one thing. Go, sell what you have and give it to the poor, and you'll have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow me.” The man's face fell at that, and he went away grieving — because he owned a great deal.

Jesus looked around and said to his disciples, “How hard it is for people with money to get into the kingdom of God.” The disciples were stunned at his words. But Jesus said to them again, “Children, how hard it is to get into the kingdom of God. It's easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to get into the kingdom of God.” They were completely astounded, and said to each other, “Then who can be saved?” Jesus looked at them and said, “For human beings it's impossible — but not for God. Everything is possible for God.”

Peter began to say to him, “Look, we've left everything and followed you.” Jesus said, “I'm telling you the truth: there is no one who has left house or brothers or sisters or mother or father or children or fields for me and for the good news who will not get back a hundred times as much — now, in this present time, houses and brothers and sisters and mothers and children and fields, along with persecutions — and in the age to come, eternal life. But many who are first will be last, and the last first.”

They were on the road going up to Jerusalem, and Jesus was walking ahead of them. They were bewildered, and the ones following were afraid. He took the twelve aside again and began telling them what was going to happen to him. “Look — we're going up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man will be handed over to the chief priests and the scribes. They will condemn him to death and hand him over to the Gentiles. They will mock him and spit on him and flog him and kill him. And after three days he will rise.”

James and John, the sons of Zebedee, came up to him and said, “Teacher, we want you to do for us whatever we ask.” He said to them, “What do you want me to do for you?” They said, “Let us sit at your right and your left in your glory.” Jesus said to them, “You have no idea what you're asking. Can you drink the cup I'm drinking? Can you be baptized with the baptism I'm being baptized with?” They said to him, “We can.” Jesus said to them, “The cup I drink, you will drink; and the baptism I'm baptized with, you will be baptized with. But to sit at my right or my left isn't mine to give. It belongs to those it has been prepared for.”

When the ten heard about it they were furious with James and John. Jesus called them over and said to them, “You know that the ones who count as rulers over the nations lord it over them, and their great men throw their weight around. It isn't like that among you. Whoever wants to be great among you must be your servant, and whoever wants to be first among you must be everyone's slave. Because even the Son of Man didn't come to be served — he came to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.”

They came to Jericho. And as he was leaving Jericho with his disciples and a large crowd, a blind beggar was sitting by the road — Bartimaeus, son of Timaeus. When he heard it was Jesus of Nazareth, he started shouting, “Jesus, son of David, have mercy on me!” Plenty of people told him to be quiet, but he shouted all the louder, “Son of David, have mercy on me!” Jesus stopped and said, “Call him.” So they called the blind man: “Take heart. Get up. He's calling you.” He threw off his coat, jumped up, and came to Jesus. Jesus said to him, “What do you want me to do for you?” The blind man said, “Rabbi, let me see again.” Jesus said to him, “Go. Your faith has saved you.” And right away he could see, and he followed him down the road.
