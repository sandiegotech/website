# The Gospel of Mark, Chapter 2

Authority, and the Company He Keeps

A few days later he came back into Capernaum, and word got out that he was home. So many people gathered that there was no room left, not even at the door, and he was speaking the word to them. Some men came, carrying a paralyzed man — four of them had him. When they couldn't get near Jesus because of the crowd, they tore open the roof above him, dug through it, and lowered the mat the paralyzed man was lying on. Jesus saw their faith and said to the man, “Son, your sins are forgiven.”

Some of the scribes were sitting there, turning it over in their minds: “Why does this man talk like that? It's blasphemy. Who can forgive sins but God alone?” Right away Jesus knew in his spirit what they were thinking, and he said to them, “Why are you turning this over in your minds? Which is easier — to say to a paralyzed man, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or to say, ‘Get up, pick up your mat, and walk’? But so you'll know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins” — he said to the paralyzed man — “I'm telling you: get up, pick up your mat, and go home.” And the man got up, picked up his mat right away, and walked out in front of all of them. They were beside themselves, and they praised God: “We have never seen anything like this.”

He went out again beside the sea. The whole crowd came to him, and he taught them. As he was walking along he saw Levi son of Alphaeus sitting at the tax booth, and he said to him, “Follow me.” And he got up and followed him.

Later, while Jesus was at the table in Levi's house, a lot of tax collectors and sinners were eating with him and his disciples — there were many of them, and they followed him. When the scribes of the Pharisees saw him eating with sinners and tax collectors, they said to his disciples, “Why does he eat with tax collectors and sinners?” Jesus heard it and said to them, “It's not the healthy who need a doctor — it's the sick. I didn't come to call the righteous. I came to call sinners.”

John's disciples and the Pharisees were fasting. People came and asked him, “Why do John's disciples fast, and the Pharisees' disciples, but yours don't?” Jesus said to them, “Can the wedding guests fast while the groom is with them? As long as they have the groom with them, they can't fast. But the days are coming when the groom will be taken away from them, and on that day they will fast.”

“Nobody sews a patch of unshrunk cloth onto an old coat. If he does, the new patch pulls away from the old, and the tear gets worse. And nobody pours new wine into old wineskins. If he does, the wine will burst the skins, and both the wine and the skins are lost. New wine goes into new wineskins.”

One Sabbath he was going through the grainfields, and as they walked his disciples began picking heads of grain. The Pharisees said to him, “Look — why are they doing what isn't allowed on the Sabbath?” He said to them, “Have you never read what David did when he was in need and hungry, he and the men with him? How he went into the house of God, when Abiathar was high priest, and ate the bread of the Presence, which only the priests are allowed to eat, and gave some to the men with him?” And he said to them, “The Sabbath was made for people, not people for the Sabbath. So the Son of Man is Lord even of the Sabbath.”
