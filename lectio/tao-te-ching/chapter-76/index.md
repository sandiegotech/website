# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 76

The Soft and the Living

When people are born they are soft and supple; when they die they are stiff and hard. So it is with all living things. Plants and trees while they live are tender and pliant; when they die they are dry and brittle.

So the firm and hard are companions of death, and the soft and yielding are companions of life.

This is why an army that will not bend does not win, and a tree that is stiff is cut down.

The hard and great take the lower place; the soft and yielding take the higher.
