# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 14

The Form of the Formless

Look for it, and you do not see it: call it the Unseen. Listen for it, and you do not hear it: call it the Unheard. Reach for it, and you cannot hold it: call it the Unfelt. These three cannot be pried apart and questioned one by one, so we blend them together and they are One.

Its upper side is not bright, its lower side is not dark. Unbroken, ceaseless, it cannot be named, and it returns again into nothing. This is the form of the formless, the image of what has no thing in it. This is the dim and the fleeting: meet it, and you do not see its face; follow it, and you do not see its back.

Hold fast to the ancient Way, and use it to master the things of the present. To know how things were in the beginning long ago — this is called the thread of the Way.
