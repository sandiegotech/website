The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 25

Something Formed Before Heaven and Earth

Chapter 2525 of 81

There was something formed out of the formless, complete in itself, that came to be before heaven and earth. Silent, and without shape, it stands alone and never changes; it moves through everything and is never worn away. It can be taken for the mother of all the world.

I do not know its name. Pressed to name it, I call it the Way; and straining further, I call it Great.

Great means flowing on without end; flowing on without end means reaching far; reaching far means returning. So the Way is great, heaven is great, earth is great, and the human being is great too. In all the world there are four that are great, and the human being is one of them.

The human being follows the earth; the earth follows heaven; heaven follows the Way; and the Way follows what is so of itself.
