# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 9

Knowing When to Stop

Better to stop in time than to fill a cup to the brim. Hammer a blade to its sharpest edge, and the edge will not last.

Fill your hall with gold and jade, and no one can keep them safe. Grow proud of wealth and rank, and you bring ruin on yourself.

When the work is done and your name is rising, withdraw. This is the Way of heaven.
