# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 81

True Words

True words are not beautiful, and beautiful words are not true. The good do not argue, and those who argue are not good. Those who truly know are not full of learning, and those full of learning do not truly know.

The sage hoards nothing. The more he does for others, the more he himself has; the more he gives to others, the more he himself gains.

The Way of heaven benefits and does not harm; the way of the sage acts and does not contend.
