# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 73

Heaven's Wide Net

Courage that shows in daring will get a man killed; courage that shows in not daring will keep him alive. One of these brings gain, the other brings harm. But why heaven turns against what it turns against — who can say? Even the sage finds it hard to know.

It is the Way of heaven not to contend, and yet it wins; not to speak, and yet it is answered; not to summon, and yet all things come of themselves. It seems to be at ease, and yet its plans never fail. Heaven's net is vast, its mesh is wide, and still nothing slips through.
