# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 72

When the People Stop Fearing

When the people no longer fear what they ought to fear, then a far greater dread comes down upon them.

Do not hem them into cramped quarters; do not bear down on what they live by.

It is only because you do not press them down that they do not grow weary of you.

And so the sage knows himself, but does not display himself; he values himself, but does not exalt himself. He lets that go and takes hold of this.
