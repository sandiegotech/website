# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 79

Holding the Left of the Tally

When a great grievance is patched up, some resentment is always left behind. How can this be called good?

And so the sage keeps the left half of the tally, and yet never presses his claim against anyone. The one who has power attends to his own side of the bargain; the one without it attends to collecting what others owe.

The Way of heaven plays no favorites; it is always on the side of the good.
