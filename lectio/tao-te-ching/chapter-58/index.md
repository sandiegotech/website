# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 58

Fortune and Misfortune

When the government is dull and unobtrusive, the people stay honest and whole. When the government is sharp and prying, the people grow crafty and broken.

Misfortune is what fortune leans on; fortune is where misfortune hides. Who knows where it all ends? There is no fixed standard. What is right turns strange, and what is good turns monstrous. Long, long has mankind been lost in this.

So the sage is square-edged but cuts no one, sharp-cornered but wounds no one, straight but never overbearing, bright but not dazzling.
