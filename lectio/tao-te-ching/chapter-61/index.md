# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 61

Lying Low

A great state is the low ground where all streams meet — the place where the world comes together, the female of the world.

The female overcomes the male by her stillness, and in her stillness she takes the lower place.

So a great state, by placing itself below a small one, wins the small state over; and a small state, by placing itself below a great one, wins the great state over. The one lies low and so gathers others in; the other lies low and so is gathered in.

A great state wants only to bring people together and nourish them; a small state wants only to be taken in and to serve. Each gets what it wants — but it is the great state that must take care to lie low.
