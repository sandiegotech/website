# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 52

The Mother and Her Children

The world had a beginning, and that beginning was the mother of all.

Once you have found the mother, you know her children. Once you know that you are her child, and hold fast to the mother in you, then to the end of your life you will meet no danger.

Stop up the openings, shut the gates, and to the end of your days your strength will never run out. Open the openings, pile up your affairs, and to the end of your days there is no saving you.

To see the small is clarity. To keep to the soft is strength.

Use the light, but turn back to clarity, and you will bring no harm upon yourself. This is what it means to live by the constant Way.
