# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 70

Coarse Cloth, Jade Within

My words are very easy to understand and very easy to put into practice, yet no one in the world can understand them or put them into practice.

My words have a source, and my deeds have a master. It is because people do not grasp this that they do not understand me.

Those who understand me are few, and that is why I am precious. So the sage wears coarse cloth on the outside but carries jade within.
