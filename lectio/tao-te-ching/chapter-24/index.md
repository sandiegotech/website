# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 24

Standing on Tiptoe

Stand on tiptoe, and you cannot keep your balance. Take great striding steps, and you cannot keep walking.

Show yourself off, and you do not shine. Insist you are right, and you do not stand out. Boast of what you have done, and the credit is not given you. Be proud of yourself, and you do not last.

Seen in the light of the Way, all this is leftover food and a tumor on the body — things that everyone finds repulsive. And so whoever holds to the Way will have nothing to do with them.
