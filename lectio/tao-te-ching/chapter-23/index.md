# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 23

Few Words Are Natural

To say little is the way of nature. A whirlwind does not last a whole morning; a downpour does not last a whole day. Who makes these things? Heaven and earth. And if even heaven and earth cannot make them last long, how much less can a person.

So one who follows the Way becomes one with the Way; one who follows its power becomes one with that power; and one who follows loss becomes one with loss.

The Way gladly receives whoever becomes one with the Way; its power gladly receives whoever becomes one with that power; and loss gladly receives whoever becomes one with loss. Where there is not trust enough, no trust is returned.
