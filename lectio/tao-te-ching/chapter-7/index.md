# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 7

Living for No Self

Heaven lasts, and earth endures. The reason heaven and earth can last and endure is that they do not live for themselves, and so they go on living.

And so the sage puts himself last, and finds himself out in front; he sets himself aside, and so is kept safe. Is it not because he is selfless that his own self is fulfilled?
