# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 12

The Belly, Not the Eye

The five colors blind the eye. The five notes deafen the ear. The five flavors numb the palate. Racing and hunting drive the heart wild, and goods that are hard to come by knock a life off its course.

And so the sage goes by the belly, not the eye. He lets that go and takes hold of this.
