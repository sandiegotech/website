# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 17

The Best Ruler Is Barely Known

The best ruler is one the people barely know is there. Next comes the ruler they love and praise. Next, the one they fear. And worst, the one they despise. Where trust runs short, it is met with distrust.

The best ruler hangs back, and is sparing of words. When the work is done and everything has run its course, the people all say: we did this ourselves.
