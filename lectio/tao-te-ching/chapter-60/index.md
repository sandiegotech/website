# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 60

Like Cooking a Small Fish

Governing a great state is like cooking a small fish.

Let the world be governed by the Way, and the spirits of the dead lose their power to haunt. It is not that they have no such power, but that their power does no harm to the people. And it is not only that their power does no harm — neither does the sage who rules do any harm.

Since neither does harm, the power of each flows back and gathers in the people.
