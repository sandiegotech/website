# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 11

The Use of Emptiness

Thirty spokes meet at a single hub, but it is the hole at the center, where there is nothing, that makes the wheel of use.

Clay is shaped into a pot, but it is the hollow inside, where there is nothing, that makes the pot of use.

Doors and windows are cut to make a room, but it is the empty space within, where there is nothing, that makes the room of use.

So what is there gives advantage; what is not there gives use.
