# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 68

The Power of Not Contending

The best warrior is not warlike. The best fighter shows no anger. The greatest conqueror does not join the battle. The one who best makes use of others puts himself beneath them.

This is the power of not contending. This is using the strength of others. This is to be matched with heaven — the highest reach of the ancients.
