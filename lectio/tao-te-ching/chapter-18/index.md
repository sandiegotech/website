# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 18

When the Way Is Lost

When the great Way is abandoned, humaneness and righteousness come forward. When cleverness and sharp wits arise, great pretense follows close behind.

When the six bonds of kin fall out of harmony, filial devotion and parental love are praised. When the state sinks into disorder, loyal ministers appear.
