The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 67

The Three Treasures

Chapter 6767 of 81

All the world says my Way is great — so great it seems good for nothing. It is precisely because it is great that it seems good for nothing. Had it looked useful, it would long ago have dwindled into something small.

But I hold three treasures, and I guard them and keep them. The first is compassion. The second is restraint. The third is not daring to put myself ahead of the world.

Because I am compassionate, I can be brave; because I am restrained, I can be generous; because I do not dare to put myself ahead of the world, I can become the leader of all. But to throw away compassion and reach for bravery, throw away restraint and reach for generosity, throw away the last place and reach for the first — that is death.

Compassion wins when it fights and holds firm when it defends. Heaven itself comes to a man's rescue, and it is with compassion that it guards him.
