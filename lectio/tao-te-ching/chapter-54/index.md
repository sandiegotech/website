# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 54

What Is Well Rooted

What is well planted cannot be uprooted. What is firmly held cannot slip away. So from generation to generation the children will keep the rites and never let the offerings to the ancestors cease.

Cultivate the Way in yourself, and your power becomes real. Cultivate it in the family, and its power overflows. Cultivate it in the village, and its power endures. Cultivate it in the state, and its power abounds. Cultivate it in the world, and its power reaches everywhere.

So look at the self through the self, the family through the family, the village through the village, the state through the state, the world through the world.

How do I know the world is like this? By this.
