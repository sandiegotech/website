# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 80

A Small Country

A small country with few people. Though they have tools that could do the work of ten or a hundred hands, let no one use them. Make the people take death seriously, and they will not move far away.

Though they have boats and carts, let no one ride in them. Though they have weapons and armor, let no one parade them.

Make the people go back to knotting cords to keep their records.

Let them find their food sweet, their clothes beautiful, their homes restful, their plain ways a delight.

The neighboring country is so close you can see it, and the crowing of its cocks and the barking of its dogs carry across to you — yet the people grow old and die without ever going back and forth.
