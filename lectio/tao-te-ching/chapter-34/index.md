# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 34

Small and Great

The great Way flows everywhere. It can reach out to the left and to the right.

All things depend on it for their life, and it turns none of them away. It does its work and lays no claim to it. It clothes and feeds all things, yet does not set itself up as their master. Forever without desire of its own, it can be called small. All things come home to it, yet it does not lord it over them, and so it can be called great.

Because it never makes itself out to be great, it is able to achieve its greatness.
