The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 62

The Refuge of All Things

Chapter 6262 of 81

The Way is the deep refuge of all things. It is the treasure of the good and the refuge of the bad.

Fine words can win honor; fine deeds can raise a person above others. And even those who are not good — why would the Way abandon them?

So when the emperor takes the throne and the three great ministers are installed, you might offer a jade disc large enough to fill both hands, sent ahead of a team of horses — but that is not as good as sitting still and offering this Way.

Why did the ancients prize the Way so highly? Was it not said: those who seek it, find it; and the guilty, through it, are forgiven? That is why it is the treasure of the world.
