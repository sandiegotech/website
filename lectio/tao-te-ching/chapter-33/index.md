# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 33

Knowing Yourself

To know others is wisdom; to know yourself is clear sight. To master others takes force; to master yourself takes true strength. To know that you have enough is to be rich. To keep going with resolve is to have will.

Whoever does not lose his place endures. Whoever dies but is not lost lives on.
