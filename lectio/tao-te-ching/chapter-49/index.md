# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 49

The Sage Has No Fixed Mind

The sage has no fixed mind of his own; he takes the mind of the people as his own.

To the good he is good; to those who are not good he is also good — and so goodness is won. The trustworthy he trusts; those who are not trustworthy he also trusts — and so trust is won.

The sage, living in the world, draws everything in and blends his mind with the world's. The people fix their eyes and ears on him, and he treats them all as his children.
