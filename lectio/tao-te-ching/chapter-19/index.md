# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 19

Return to the Uncarved Block

Renounce sageliness, throw out cleverness, and the people will be better off a hundredfold. Renounce humaneness, throw out righteousness, and the people will return to devotion within their families and care for one another. Renounce ingenuity, throw out the chase after gain, and there will be no more thieves or bandits.

These three are mere ornament, and ornament is not enough. So let the people hold to something solid: see the plain and undyed, hold to the uncarved block, think little of self, and want little.
