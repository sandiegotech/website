# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 43

The Softest Overcomes the Hardest

The softest thing in the world rides down the hardest. What has no substance slips in where there is no opening. By this I know the worth of acting without forcing.

The teaching that uses no words, the good that comes of acting without forcing — few in the world ever reach them.
