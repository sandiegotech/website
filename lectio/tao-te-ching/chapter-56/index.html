The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 56

Those Who Know Do Not Speak

Chapter 5656 of 81

Those who know do not speak; those who speak do not know.

Block the openings, shut the gates. Blunt the sharp edges, untie the tangles, soften the glare, settle into the dust. This is called the mysterious union.

Such a one cannot be drawn close or held at a distance, cannot be helped or harmed, cannot be honored or disgraced. And so such a one is honored by all the world.
