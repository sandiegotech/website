# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 35

Hold the Great Image

Hold fast to the great image, and the whole world comes to you. It comes and takes no harm, but finds rest, peace, and ease.

Music and fine food make the passing traveler stop. But when the Way is put into words, it is bland and without flavor. Look for it, and there is nothing to see; listen for it, and there is nothing to hear; yet use it, and it is never used up.
