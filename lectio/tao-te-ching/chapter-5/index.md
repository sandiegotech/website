# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 5

Straw Dogs

Heaven and earth are not humane: they treat the ten thousand things as straw dogs. The sage is not humane: he treats the people as straw dogs.

The space between heaven and earth — is it not like a bellows? Empty, yet it never runs out; work it, and it gives out all the more.

Too many words run dry. Better to keep to the center.
