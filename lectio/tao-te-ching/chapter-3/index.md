# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 3

Keeping the Heart Unstirred

Do not exalt the worthy, and the people will not compete with one another. Do not prize goods that are hard to come by, and the people will not turn to theft. Do not put on display the things that stir desire, and the heart of the people will stay calm.

And so, when the sage governs, he empties their minds and fills their bellies, weakens their ambition and strengthens their bones. He keeps the people without knowledge and without craving, so that the clever never dare to act. Act without forcing, and nothing is left ungoverned.
