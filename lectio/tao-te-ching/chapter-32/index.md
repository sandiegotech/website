# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 32

The Uncarved Block

The Way is eternal and has no name. The uncarved block, though small, is something no one in the world can master. If kings and lords could only hold to it, all things would come to them of their own accord.

Heaven and earth would join and let fall a sweet dew, and the people, with no one to command them, would fall into order by themselves.

Once things are cut and shaped, there are names. And once there are names, you must know when to stop. Knowing when to stop is how you come to no harm.

The Way is to the world what the sea is to the rivers and streams — everything flows down to it and home.
