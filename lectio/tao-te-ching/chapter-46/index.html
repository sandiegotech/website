The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 46

Knowing You Have Enough

Chapter 4646 of 81

When the Way prevails in the world, the swift horses are sent back to haul dung for the fields. When the Way is cast aside, war-horses are bred out along the borders.

There is no calamity greater than not knowing when you have enough; there is no fault greater than the craving to get more. So the contentment of one who knows he has enough is contentment that lasts.
