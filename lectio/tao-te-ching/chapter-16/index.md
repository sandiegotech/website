# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 16

Returning to the Root

Reach all the way to emptiness. Hold fast to stillness. The ten thousand things rise and stir, and I watch them return. For all their teeming growth, each one comes back to its root.

Returning to the root is stillness; this is called returning to what was fated. Returning to what was fated is the constant; to know the constant is to see clearly. Not to know it is to act blindly and bring on disaster.

Know the constant, and you grow all-embracing. All-embracing, you are impartial; impartial, you are kingly; kingly, you are like heaven; like heaven, you are one with the Way. One with the Way, you last long, and to the end of your life you come to no harm.
