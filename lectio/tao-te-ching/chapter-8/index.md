# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 8

Like Water

The highest good is like water. Water benefits all things and does not contend with them; it settles in the low places that everyone else scorns. And so it is close to the Way.

In dwelling, it holds to the ground. In the heart, it holds to depth. In giving, it holds to kindness. In speech, it holds to truth. In governing, it holds to good order. In handling affairs, it holds to skill. In moving, it holds to the right moment.

It is just because it does not contend that it is without fault.
