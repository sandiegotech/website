The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 41

How the Way Is Heard

Chapter 4141 of 81

When the best student hears about the Way, he sets to work and practices it with all his heart. When the middling student hears about it, he half keeps it and half lets it go. When the worst student hears about it, he laughs out loud. And if he did not laugh, it would not be the Way.

So the old sayings have it: The bright Way looks dark. The Way that goes forward looks as if it were going back. The smooth Way looks rough. The highest power looks empty, like a valley. The purest white looks stained. The broadest power looks as if it falls short. Firm, settled power looks flimsy. What is most real looks as if it could change. The great square has no corners. The great vessel is the last to be finished. The great sound can barely be heard. The great image has no shape.

The Way is hidden and has no name. Yet it is the Way alone that gives all things what they need and brings them to completion.
