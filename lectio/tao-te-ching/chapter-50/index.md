# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 50

No Room for Death

We come out into life and go back into death.

Three in ten are companions of life. Three in ten are companions of death. And three in ten are those who live, yet move into the place of death. Why? Because they clutch too hard at living.

But I have heard that one who truly knows how to live can cross the land without meeting rhinoceros or tiger, and pass through battle without armor or weapon. The rhinoceros finds no place to gore him, the tiger no place to sink its claws, the blade no place to drive its point. Why? Because in him there is no room for death.
