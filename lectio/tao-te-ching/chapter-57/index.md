# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 57

Win the World by Not Meddling

Govern the state with what is straight and ordinary. Wage war with surprise. But win the world by not meddling.

How do I know it works this way? By this. The more prohibitions there are in the land, the poorer the people grow. The more sharp weapons the people have, the deeper the state falls into disorder. The more clever and cunning people become, the more strange things start to happen. The more laws and decrees are posted up, the more thieves and robbers there are.

So the sage says: I act without forcing, and the people transform themselves. I love stillness, and the people right themselves. I do not meddle, and the people grow prosperous on their own. I have no desires, and the people return on their own to the uncarved block.
