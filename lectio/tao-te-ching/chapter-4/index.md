# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 4

The Bottomless Well

The Way is empty, yet however much you use it, it is never used up. It is so deep — it seems to be the ancestor of the ten thousand things.

It blunts the sharp edges, unties the knots, softens the glare, settles down with the dust. So still, so hidden, yet it seems to be there.

I do not know whose child it is. It seems to be older than God.
