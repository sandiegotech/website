# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 10

Mysterious Power

Can you hold body and soul in a single embrace and never let them part? Can you gather your breath and make it soft and supple, soft as a newborn child's? Can you cleanse the dark mirror of your mind until it is without a flaw?

Can you love the people and govern the state, and do it without scheming? When the gate of heaven opens and shuts, can you play the part of the female? Can your understanding reach into every corner, and yet seem to know nothing at all?

To give life and to nourish it; to give life and not possess it; to do the work and not lay claim to it; to lead, and not to lord it over what you lead — this is what is called the mysterious power.
