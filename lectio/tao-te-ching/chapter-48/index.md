# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 48

Less and Less

In the pursuit of learning, something is added every day. In the pursuit of the Way, something is dropped every day. Dropped, and dropped again, until you come to acting without forcing. Act without forcing, and nothing is left undone.

The world is always won by leaving it alone. Once you start to meddle, you are not fit to win the world.
