# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 66

Why Rivers and Seas Are Kings

Rivers and seas are kings of the hundred valley streams because they lie below them — that is why they are their kings. So one who would rise above the people must, in speech, put himself below them; one who would lead them must put himself behind them.

In this way the sage is above the people, yet they feel no weight; he is ahead of them, yet they feel no harm.

So the whole world gladly pushes him forward and never tires of him. Because he does not contend, no one in the world can contend with him.
