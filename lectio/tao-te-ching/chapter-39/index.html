The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 39

Those That Attained the One

Chapter 3939 of 81

From of old, these are the things that attained the One: heaven attained it and became clear; earth attained it and became firm; the spirits attained it and became holy; the valleys attained it and became full; all living things attained it and lived; rulers and kings attained it and became the standard for the world. It is the One that made them so.

Without it, heaven would have nothing to keep it clear, and would crack. Earth would have nothing to keep it firm, and would quake. The spirits would have nothing to make them holy, and would fade. The valleys would have nothing to keep them full, and would run dry. All living things would have nothing to keep them alive, and would die out. Rulers and kings would have nothing to keep them high, and would fall.

So the noble takes the humble as its root, and the high takes the low as its foundation. That is why rulers and kings call themselves orphaned, widowed, unworthy. Is this not because they hold the humble to be their root? Take a carriage apart piece by piece, and there is no carriage left to find. So do not wish to glitter like jade; be plain like common stone.
