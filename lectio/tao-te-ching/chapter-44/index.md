# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 44

Your Name or Your Self

Your name or your self — which is dearer? Your self or your wealth — which matters more? Gaining or losing — which does you the greater harm?

So the more you love, the more it costs you; the more you hoard, the heavier the loss when it goes.

Know what is enough, and you meet no disgrace. Know when to stop, and you meet no danger. And so you endure.
