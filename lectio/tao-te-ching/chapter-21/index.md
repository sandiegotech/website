# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 21

The Essence Within

The form of great power flows from the Way alone.

And the Way itself, as a thing, is elusive and vague. Vague and elusive, yet within it are images. Elusive and vague, yet within it are things. Deep and dark, yet within it is an essence that is real; and this essence can be trusted.

From of old until now its name has never passed away, and by it we know the origin of all things. How do I know the way of all things at their origin? By this.
