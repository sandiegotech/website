# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 74

The Master Carpenter

If the people do not fear death, why threaten them with it? But suppose the people always feared death, and whenever someone did wrong I could seize him and put him to death — who then would dare?

There is always the great executioner whose place it is to kill. To kill in place of that great executioner is to hew wood in place of the master carpenter — and whoever hews in the master carpenter's place will hardly fail to cut his own hand.
