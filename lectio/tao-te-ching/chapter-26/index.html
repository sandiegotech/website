The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 26

The Heavy and the Still

Chapter 2626 of 81

The heavy is the root of the light; stillness masters restlessness.

And so the sage travels all day without leaving the baggage wagon behind. Splendid sights may rise before him, yet he keeps his place, calm and aloof. How then should the lord of ten thousand chariots carry himself lightly before the world? To be light is to lose the root; to be restless is to lose your mastery.
