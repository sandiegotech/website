# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 63

The Hard Begins Easy

Act without forcing. Work without fuss. Taste what has no flavor. Make the small great and the few many. Repay resentment with kindness.

Tackle the hard thing while it is still easy; do the great thing while it is still small. Every hard thing in the world begins easy, and every great thing begins small. So the sage never reaches for greatness, and that is how he achieves greatness.

A promise given lightly wins little trust, and treating everything as easy ends in much hardship. So the sage treats even easy things as hard, and in the end nothing is hard to him.
