# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 29

The Sacred Vessel

Whoever wants to take the world and shape it to his will, I see that he will not succeed. The world is a sacred vessel, not to be tampered with. Tamper with it, and you ruin it; grasp at it, and you lose it.

For things go their own way: some lead, others follow; some breathe gently, others hard; some are strong, others weak; some rise up, others fall down.

And so the sage casts off excess, casts off extravagance, casts off pride.
