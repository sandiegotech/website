# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 37

Nothing Left Undone

The Way never forces anything, yet nothing is left undone.

If lords and kings could hold to it, all things would change of themselves.

And if, as they changed, desire began to stir, I would still it with the nameless block of uncarved wood.

The nameless uncarved block: it too is free of desire. Without desire, there is stillness, and the world settles of itself.
