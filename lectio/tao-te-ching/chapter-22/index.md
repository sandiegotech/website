# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 22

Yield and Remain Whole

Yield, and remain whole. Bend, and stay straight. Empty yourself, and be filled. Wear out, and be made new. Have little, and you will gain; have much, and you will be confused.

And so the sage holds to the One, and becomes a model for the world. He does not display himself, and so he shines. He does not insist he is right, and so he stands out. He does not boast, and so he is given credit. He is not proud, and so he endures. It is because he does not contend that no one in the world can contend with him.

The old saying — yield, and remain whole — how could that be empty words? Truly it keeps you whole, and brings you home.
