# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 65

Ruling Without Cleverness

The ancients who were good at the Way did not use it to make the people clever, but to keep them simple.

People are hard to govern because they know too much. So to rule a state by cleverness is to rob the state; to rule a state without cleverness is its blessing.

To know these two is to know the pattern. Always to know the pattern is what is called the mysterious power. The mysterious power runs deep, it reaches far, and it turns back with all things until they come round to the great accord.
