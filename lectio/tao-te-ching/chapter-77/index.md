# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 77

Drawing the Bow

The Way of heaven is like drawing a bow. What is high is pressed down, and what is low is lifted up; where there is too much, it takes away, and where there is too little, it fills in.

This is the Way of heaven: it takes from what has excess and gives to what falls short. The way of human beings is not so. It takes from those who have too little to add to those who already have too much.

Who has more than enough, and can offer it to the whole world? Only one who holds to the Way.

And so the sage acts, but does not rely on what he has done; he accomplishes the work, but does not dwell in it. He has no wish to be seen as better than the rest.
