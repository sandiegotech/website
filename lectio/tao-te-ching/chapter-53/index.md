# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 53

The Robber's Boast

If I had the smallest scrap of sense, I would walk the great Way, and fear only one thing — that I might stray from it.

The great Way is smooth and easy, but people love the bypaths.

The courts are swept spotless while the fields choke with weeds and the granaries stand empty. Yet some wear embroidered robes, carry sharp swords at the hip, gorge themselves on food and drink, and hoard more wealth than they could ever use. This is the robber's boast, not the Way.
