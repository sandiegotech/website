The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 59

Sparing

Chapter 5959 of 81

In governing people and in serving heaven, nothing is better than restraint.

It is only by restraint that you yield early to the Way; and yielding early means building up an ever deeper store of power. With that deep store of power, there is nothing you cannot overcome; and when there is nothing you cannot overcome, no one can know your limit. The one whose limit no one knows is fit to hold the state.

Hold to the mother of the state, and you may last long. This is what it means to have deep roots and a firm base — the way of long life and lasting vision.
