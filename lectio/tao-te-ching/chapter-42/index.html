The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 42

The Way Bore the One

Chapter 4242 of 81

The Way bore the One; the One bore the Two; the Two bore the Three; and the Three bore the ten thousand things. All things carry yin on their backs and hold yang in their arms, and the blending of these breaths makes their harmony.

There is nothing people hate more than to be called orphaned, widowed, or unworthy — yet these are the very names that kings and lords take for themselves. For some things gain by being made less, and some things lose by being made more.

What others teach, I also teach: the violent and overbearing do not die a natural death. I take this as the father of my teaching.
