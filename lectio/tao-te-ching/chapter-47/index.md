# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 47

Knowing Without Going Out

Without going out the door, you can know the world. Without looking through the window, you can see the Way of heaven. The farther you go, the less you know.

And so the sage knows without going about, understands and names things without seeing them, and accomplishes without acting.
