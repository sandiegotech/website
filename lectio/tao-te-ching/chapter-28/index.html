The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 28

Know the Masculine, Keep to the Feminine

Chapter 2828 of 81

Know the masculine, but keep to the feminine, and be the ravine of the world. Be the ravine of the world, and the constant power will never leave you; you return to the state of a newborn child.

Know the white, but keep to the black, and be the model for the world. Be the model for the world, and the constant power will never fail you; you return to the limitless.

Know honor, but keep to disgrace, and be the valley of the world. Be the valley of the world, and the constant power will be complete in you; you return to the uncarved block.

When the uncarved block is split, it is made into vessels. The sage, put to use, becomes the chief of officials. And so the greatest carving cuts nothing away.
