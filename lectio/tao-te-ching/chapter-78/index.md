# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 78

Nothing Softer Than Water

Nothing in the world is softer or weaker than water, and yet for wearing away the hard and the strong nothing surpasses it; nothing can take its place.

That the weak overcomes the strong, and the soft overcomes the hard — everyone in the world knows it, yet no one can put it into practice.

And so the sage has said: the one who bears the country's shame is fit to be lord of its altars; the one who bears the country's misfortunes is fit to be king of the world.

True words seem to say the opposite.
