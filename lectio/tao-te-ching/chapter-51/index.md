# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 51

The Way Gives Life, Power Nurtures

The Way gives all things their life, and its power nurtures them. Matter gives them their shapes, and circumstance brings them to completion. So there is nothing among all things that does not honor the Way and value its power.

This honoring of the Way and valuing of its power is commanded by no one. It is always so of itself.

For the Way gives all things life and its power nurtures them: it rears them and raises them, it shelters them and ripens them, it feeds them and shields them.

It gives them life and does not possess them; it does the work and lays no claim to it; it leads, and does not lord it over what it leads. This is what is called the mysterious power.
