# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 71

Knowing That You Do Not Know

To know that you do not know is best; to not know, and yet think you know, is a flaw.

It is only by recognizing this flaw as a flaw that one can be free of it. The sage is free of it because he recognizes the flaw as a flaw, and so it is no flaw in him.
