# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 69

The Side That Grieves Wins

There is a saying among soldiers: "I dare not be the host, but would rather be the guest; I dare not advance an inch, but would rather fall back a foot." This is called marching forward where there is no march to make, rolling up the sleeve where there is no arm to bare, taking up the weapon where there is no weapon to grasp, facing the enemy where there is no enemy to face.

There is no disaster greater than underestimating the enemy; to underestimate the enemy is to come close to losing my treasures. So when armies are drawn up against each other and the weapons cross, it is the side that grieves that wins.
