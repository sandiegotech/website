# The Tao Te Ching, Chapter 13

Favor and Disgrace

Favor and disgrace are both alarming. Honor great trouble as you honor your own body.

What does it mean that favor and disgrace are both alarming? Favor is a lowly thing: to win it is alarming, and to lose it is alarming. This is what it means that favor and disgrace are both alarming. And what does it mean to honor great trouble as you honor your own body? The reason I can suffer great trouble is that I have a body; if I had no body, what trouble could reach me?

So the one who honors the world as his own body can be given the world to hold; the one who loves the world as his own body can be trusted with the world.
