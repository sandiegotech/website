The Dhammapada, Chapter XIII

The World

Chapter XIII13 of 26

167. Do not follow the low way. Do not live in heedlessness. Do not take up false views. Do not be a friend of the world.

168. Rouse yourself! Do not be idle! Follow the path of virtue. The virtuous live at ease, in this world and the next.

169. Follow the path of virtue, not the way of wrongdoing. The virtuous live at ease, in this world and the next.

170. Look on the world as a bubble; look on it as a mirage. The King of Death never sees the one who sees the world this way.

171. Come, look at this glittering world, like a painted royal chariot. Fools sink into it, but the wise take no hold of it.

172. The one who was heedless before, and afterward grows heedful, lights up this world like the moon set free from a cloud.

173. The one whose evil deeds are covered over by good lights up this world like the moon set free from a cloud.

174. This world is blind; only a few here see clearly. Only a few reach heaven, like birds slipping free of the net.

175. Swans travel the path of the sun; the adept travel through the air by their own spiritual power. The wise are led out of this world once they have conquered Mara and all his army.

176. The one who breaks the single law of truth, who speaks lies and mocks the world to come, will stop at no evil.

177. The stingy never reach the world of the gods; only fools have no praise for giving. The wise rejoice in giving, and so find happiness in the world to come.

178. Better than sole rule over the earth, better than going to heaven, better than lordship over all the worlds, is the fruit of the first step into the stream.
