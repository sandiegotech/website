The Dhammapada, Chapter VIII

The Thousands

Chapter VIII8 of 26

100. Better than a thousand words, if they are empty of sense, is the one word of sense that brings peace to whoever hears it.

101. Better than a thousand verses, if they are empty of sense, is the one line of verse that brings peace to whoever hears it.

102. Better than reciting a hundred verses empty of sense is the one word of the teaching that brings peace to whoever hears it.

103. Though a man should conquer a thousand thousand men in battle, the one who conquers himself is the greatest conqueror of all.

104. Victory over oneself is better than victory over anyone else. The man who has mastered himself and lives always under restraint—

105. not a god, not a Gandharva, not Mara, not even Brahma could turn his victory into defeat.

106. Though month after month for a hundred years a man should offer a thousand sacrifices, if for a single moment he honors one who has perfected himself, that homage is worth more than a hundred years of sacrifice.

107. Though for a hundred years a man should tend the sacred fire in the forest, if for a single moment he honors one who has perfected himself, that homage is worth more than a hundred years of worship.

108. Whatever a man offers or sacrifices in this world for a whole year to gain merit, all of it is not worth a quarter of the reverence shown to the upright; reverence to the upright is better.

109. To one who always honors and respects the elders, four things increase: long life, beauty, happiness, and strength.

110. Better than a hundred years lived in vice, with no self-control, is a single day lived in virtue and reflection.

111. Better than a hundred years lived in ignorance, with no self-control, is a single day lived in wisdom and reflection.

112. Better than a hundred years lived in idleness and weakness is a single day lived with firm and steady effort.

113. Better than a hundred years lived without ever seeing how all things rise and pass away is a single day in which you see them rise and pass away.

114. Better than a hundred years lived without ever seeing the deathless is a single day in which you see the deathless.

115. Better than a hundred years lived without ever seeing the highest law is a single day in which you see the highest law.
